Adventure Awaits

There is never a dull moment in Nyanga. Explore the wild beauty right outside your door.

Chase the Waterfalls

Take a short drive to the magnificent Nyangombe Falls. Feel the mist on your face and listen to the roar of the water crashing down the rocks—a perfect photo opportunity and a refreshing escape.

Conquer the Peaks

Lace up your hiking boots and explore the trails leading to Nyangani, Zimbabwe's highest peak. The panoramic views from the top are absolutely breathtaking and worth every step.

Experience the Skywalk

For the ultimate adventure, try the skywalk experience. Walk on a suspended platform high above the ground and enjoy stunning views of the surrounding landscape.
